Frequently asked questions

Does SynAppse replace the business owner?

No. It handles repetitive questions and initial qualification, then hands off conversations that need judgment or a personal touch.

What happens when it does not know the answer?

We define clear human-handoff rules. It is not supposed to make up an answer.

Who is this for?

Service businesses that receive recurring WhatsApp inquiries and want faster response without adding staff. If you receive very few inquiries or have a highly complex sales process, we will first check whether it is worth it.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There is no long-term commitment. The monthly fee covers operation and maintenance.

Want to know if it fits your business?

Tell me what your business does and roughly how many inquiries you get each month. I will give you a straight answer, even if SynAppse is not the right fit.
